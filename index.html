About

Hi, I'm Barry Rubio

I'm eighteen, from Guihulngan City in Negros Oriental. I started with web development out of plain curiosity — changing one line and watching the page respond — and it has held my attention since.

I'm a Computer Systems Servicing NC II passer, which gave me a working standard for the technical side of the trade rather than just the theory. These days I'm a first-year student at Colegio de Santa Rita de San Carlos, Inc., building small pages between classes and learning the parts of the craft that don't show up in a tutorial.

What I care about is unglamorous: pages that load quickly, read clearly, and hold together on a phone in the province as well as on a desktop anywhere.
